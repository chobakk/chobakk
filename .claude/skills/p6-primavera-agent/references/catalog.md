# فهرست workflowهای P6 (۱۵ مورد)

هر workflow در `references/workflows/<نام>/SKILL.md` است. همگی به ابزارهای `p6_*` سرور «MCP for Primavera P6» نیاز دارند.

| workflow | کار | ابزارها | نوشتن در دیتابیس |
|---|---|---|---|
| `baseline-variance` | Variance of a P6 project against its baseline or another project: finish variance, slip causes, the activities that moved most, logic changes and earned value at the data date. | p6_compare_projects, p6_progress_curve, p6_project_summary | نه (فقط خواندن) |
| `delay-whatif` | What-if impact of delaying one activity of a P6 project, computed in memory: the activity today, what drives it, where its float comes from, then the project finish, milestones, critical counts and the activities that move (the database is never written). | p6_driving_path, p6_float_origin, p6_get_activity, p6_schedule_whatif, p6_update_activity | فقط در حافظه محاسبه می‌شود؛ دیتابیس نوشته نمی‌شود |
| `export-dashboard` | Excel project controls dashboard of a P6 project (one A3 page and detail sheets), then a short summary of its cards, counts, failed DCMA checks and warnings (the database is never written). | p6_export_dashboard, p6_project_summary | نه (فقط خواندن) |
| `float-near-critical` | Total float profile of a P6 project: float statistics, distribution by range, the float paths of the last F9 and the near-critical activities (total float above 0 and within a few days). | p6_critical_path, p6_float_analysis, p6_float_paths | نه (فقط خواندن) |
| `list` | List the projects of the database grouped by EPS, with status, data date and finish (template projects and baselines left out). | p6_list_eps, p6_list_projects | نه (فقط خواندن) |
| `logic-date-integrity` | Checks that the stored dates of a P6 project follow from its logic, durations and schedule options: this server's CPM engine against the last F9, the activities whose dates differ with their probable cause, and the schedule options in force (read only). Use before pressing F9 on an imported update, or when the user asks whether the submitted dates can be trusted. | p6_cpm_validate, p6_schedule_options | نه (فقط خواندن) |
| `lookahead-brief` | Short look-ahead of a P6 project: activities in progress, starting and finishing in the next weeks, critical ones first, by week of the window, with fixed follow-up actions. | p6_lookahead | نه (فقط خواندن) |
| `milestone-tracker` | Milestones of a P6 project against its baseline: dates, variance in working and calendar days, float and critical flags, the open milestones most late and what drives the first one; with contract milestones given, their constraints, predecessors and float origin (the database is never written). | p6_driving_path, p6_milestones | نه (فقط خواندن) |
| `progress-earned-value` | Progress of a P6 project in units: % complete by every method, earned value and SPI at the data date, the S-curve per month or week against the baseline, and progress by WBS. | p6_list_wbs, p6_progress_curve, p6_project_summary | نه (فقط خواندن) |
| `resource-wbs-summary` | Units and costs of a P6 project by resource, then activities and units by WBS and by activity status, with totals. | p6_group_summary, p6_resource_summary | نه (فقط خواندن) |
| `schedule-quality-audit` | DCMA 14 quality audit of a P6 project: score and weighted grade, checks against their thresholds, engine check, trend since the previous audit, dangling ends, redundant links and float profile, with fixes proposed only. | p6_add_relationships, p6_cpm_validate, p6_dangling, p6_delete_relationships, p6_float_analysis, p6_float_origin, p6_health_check, p6_health_check_compare, p6_redundant_links | فقط پیشنهاد اصلاح؛ بدون نوشتن |
| `schedule-risk` | Monte Carlo schedule risk of a P6 project on this server's CPM engine: finish dates at p10, p50, p80 and p90, the chance to finish on time, the p80 buffer, milestones, the activities that drive the finish most often and the assumptions (read only). | p6_cpm_validate, p6_schedule_risk | نه (فقط خواندن) |
| `update-import-safely` | Import an updated XER into an existing P6 project safely: inspect the file, dry run the update, report what it would change and what the XER lacks, then apply only on the user's explicit yes. | p6_import_xer, p6_inspect_xer | نوشتن (p6_import_xer؛ ابتدا dry run و تأیید صریح) |
| `update-integrity-audit` | Integrity audit of a P6 schedule update against the previous update: open ends tied to the finish, constraints removed to clear negative float, must finish date moved, actual dates rewritten, durations cut, critical links removed, link types, lags, options and calendars changed, activities deleted, data date, with the questions to raise with the contractor (read only). | p6_compare_projects, p6_update_integrity | نه (فقط خواندن) |
| `weekly-update-review` | Weekly schedule update review of a P6 project: progress, finish dates, critical path, slippage against the baseline, changes since the previous update when given, progress issues and a critical look-ahead, with fixed actions (read only). | p6_compare_projects, p6_critical_path, p6_lookahead, p6_progress_check, p6_project_summary | نه (فقط خواندن) |
